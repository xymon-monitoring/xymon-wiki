# Project organisation

Xymon is maintained by its contributors. What follows is how rights are held in
the `xymon-monitoring` organisation on GitHub, and what holding one asks of the
person who holds it. Who holds what is not listed here.

## Groups

A group grants a right and asks for the work behind it.

| Group | Grants | Expects |
|---|---|---|
| **Organisation owner** | admin on every repository, the organisation's settings and membership | answering for who holds what, and keeping that list short enough to review |
| **maintainers** | maintain on `xymon`, and the only bypass of its ruleset | reviewing others' changes; bypassing only where [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/xymon-monitoring/xymon/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) allows |
| **contributors** | push on `xymon` | opening pull requests and seeing them through review |

Anyone can contribute without belonging to a group: open an issue, or a pull
request from a fork — see
[First contribution](../contributing/git/first-contribution.md).

Being an organisation owner is a role in the organisation, not a team, and it
comes with admin on every repository by itself. A member who is not an owner
has no access to a repository until a team grants it.

## Holding a right

- **Identified and contactable.** Anyone holding a right in the organisation
  can be tied to a person and reached. A right nobody can ask about cannot be
  reviewed or revoked in good faith.
- **Held for the work.** A right that nobody exercises is not harmless: it is a
  gap someone else fills quietly. Holding a group is a decision, not an
  accumulation, and groups follow actual involvement as it changes.
- **Changed traceably.** Rights are granted and removed on the record, so that a
  change of access is never silent.

## Decisions

Discussion happens on the mailing list and the other channels listed on
[Project & community](project-and-community.md). A decision becomes
authoritative when it is recorded on GitHub, in the issue or pull request that
took it.
